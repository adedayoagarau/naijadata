# Red Flag Detection Guide

## Vague Description Keywords (Flag if >₦50M)
- Miscellaneous, Sundry, Other expenses
- Contingency, Welfare packages
- Stakeholder engagement, Sensitization
- Empowerment (without beneficiary details)

## Budget Padding Indicators
- YoY increase >100% without policy change
- New agencies without legal basis
- Duplicate projects across MDAs
- Items not in executive proposal

## Travel Red Flags
- International travel > Local travel
- Travel > Service delivery budget
- State Assembly travel > ₦300M
- Per-legislator travel > ₦20M

## Procurement Red Flags
- Emergency procurement for routine items
- Contract splitting (multiple <₦50M to same vendor)
- Single-source without justification
- Variation orders >15% of contract

## High-Risk Budget Codes
- 22020803: International Travel Training
- 22020804: International Travel Others
- 22021010: Other Miscellaneous
- 22020501: Consultancy
- 22021002: Honorarium/Sitting Allowance
