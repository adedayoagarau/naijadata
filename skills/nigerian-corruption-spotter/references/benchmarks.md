# Nigerian Budget Benchmarks

## Infrastructure Costs (2024-2025 Naira)

| Type | Cost | Red Flag Above |
|------|------|----------------|
| Primary school (6 classrooms) | ₦150M | ₦300M |
| Primary Health Center | ₦150M | ₦300M |
| Borehole | ₦5M | ₦10M |
| Road (asphalt, per km) | ₦150M | ₦350M |
| Road (rural, per km) | ₦50M | ₦100M |
| Affordable house | ₦15M | ₦30M |

## Travel Benchmarks

## Legislature Costs

| Level | Per-Legislator Normal | Red Flag |
|-------|----------------------|----------|
| State Assembly | ₦30-50M | >₦100M |
| House of Reps | ₦80-120M | >₦200M |
| Senate | ₦100-150M | >₦250M |

## Sector Spending Targets

| Sector | Target % of Budget |
|--------|-------------------|
| Education | 15-20% (UNESCO) |
| Health | 15% (Abuja Declaration) |
| Capital Expenditure | 30-40% |

## Ratio Red Flags

- Overhead > Capital (development MDAs)
- Travel > 25% of Overhead
- Admin > 30% of Total
- Personnel > 70% of Budget
- "Miscellaneous" > ₦100M
