## Common Methods
1. Budget padding (unauthorized items)
2. Contract inflation (above market rates)
3. Ghost workers/projects
4. Contract splitting (avoid thresholds)
5. Emergency procurement abuse
6. Security vote opacity
7. Diversion of funds

## High-Risk MDAs
- NNPC (oil revenue)
- Defense (classification)
- National Assembly (self-appropriation)
- NDDC (₦6T spent, minimal impact)
- State Assemblies
- Governor's Office (security votes)
